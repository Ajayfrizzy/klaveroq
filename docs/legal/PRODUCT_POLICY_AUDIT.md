The brief identifies RumptyCloud application hosting and Supabase PostgreSQL, confirms the community beta and says real payments, government-ID verification and uploads are not operational. The repository proves implementation and configuration options, not which optional providers are live. `.env` was not read or changed. No applicable specialist skill was available for this task; `apps/web/AGENTS.md` and the installed Next.js page/metadata guides were read.

## Acceptance audit and decision

`jobs.termsAcceptedAt` is invitation-linked agreement consent; `fee_quotes.policyVersion` identifies a calculation policy. Neither records general legal-document acceptance. Registration has no general policy-version acceptance schema. M3 adds explicit document versions/status and does **not** collect acceptance, infer acceptance from continued use or backfill existing users. No database migration is justified for drafts.

Future rollout requires immutable approved versions, exact accepted version and timestamp, appropriate account reference, scoped consent purpose, notice for existing users, legal decisions on assent/reacceptance, a reviewed additive migration and disposable-database tests. Do not use agreement acceptance or marketing preferences as a substitute. Do not collect IP/device evidence solely for acceptance without a reviewed need.

## M3 corrections

The existing deletion UI said personal data “is removed” after 30 days even though cleanup is disabled. M3 changes only that explanation to conditional processing and explicitly states the beta limitation. The legacy client RouteTitle component otherwise overwrote legal document metadata; legal routes now retain their server-owned titles. All operational flags, security checks, schemas and payment integrations remain unchanged.
